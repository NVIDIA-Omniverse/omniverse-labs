Timeline (NLE)

The editor is the strip below the viewport — one track per variant set plus a camera track. Drag its top edge to resize. Hide unused tracks with the track's −; bring them back from show hidden….

On a track, pick a variant and Append. The toolbar append toggle controls whether it stacks after the last clip or drops at the playhead. Change a clip's variant with the ▾ on the clip; drag to move, drag the right edge to resize. To delete, select a clip and press Delete (or use Delete clip in the toolbar).

Drag the ruler to scrub — the viewport previews the look live. Set an output folder and Render to MP4; the result lands in Results.

Presets: Mixer fills every set's track in parallel; Slideshow steps through one change at a time (a staircase across the per-set tracks); Clear empties all clips.
