# NVIDIA logo sculpture

The eye and NVIDIA wordmark outlines are extracted from the SVG displayed on [NVIDIA's official logo and brand page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/legal-info/logo-brand-usage/).

The 2D outlines retain their original proportions and are tessellated and extruded for the central gallery sculpture. Source URL, original vector and conversion provenance are included alongside this notice.
