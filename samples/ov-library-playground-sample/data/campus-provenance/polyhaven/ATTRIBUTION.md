The original provenance records are preserved without modification and include
download URLs, source hashes and authorship. Their source hashes describe the
downloaded textures. The campus uses adapted oak color and roughness maps;
the other seven channels are unchanged. [Texture derivations](texture-derivations.json)
map the original hashes to the shipped files and document the oak adjustments.

This folder is bundled as `provenance/polyhaven/` inside the campus archive.
Keep it with the campus when copying or redistributing that archive. CC0 applies
to the named Poly Haven textures; other campus content retains its own terms.
